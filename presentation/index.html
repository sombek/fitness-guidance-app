Stakeholder discussion draftVersion 01 · 6 September 2026

Phase 1.
The scope, clearly.

Move the coaching business’s 50–100 existing active clients from spreadsheets into a client mobile app and a coach web dashboard, covering training, food, and progress.

This phase replaces the agreed coaching workflow, not every spreadsheet feature. The sections below combine agreed direction with explicitly marked proposals and decisions. They are not a final development commitment.

Users & accessTrainingFoodProgressMigrationBoundariesRelease criteriaDecisions
01

Users, access & onboarding

UserPlatformAccess
Head coachWeb dashboardAll clients; assigns clients to coaches and manages plans.
CoachWeb dashboardOnly assigned clients, their plans, and progress.
ClientMobile appOnly their own plans and records.

Agreed foundation

  • Invitation-only registration by email; no public signup.
  • Client assignment and a consolidated view of training, food, and progress.

Proposed operational essentials

Invitation status and resending, client search, reassignment, account recovery, and client-access deactivation.

Decision required

Launch on iOS, Android, or both? Arabic-first or bilingual? Confirm who can invite clients and maintain coach accounts.

02

Training

Coach dashboard

  • Browse the existing exercise library.
  • Create, update, and assign training plans using library exercises.
  • Review each client’s workout-completion history.
  • Proposed: basic add/edit controls to maintain the library.

Client mobile app

  • View the assigned plan and exercise instructions.
  • Open exercise video links.
  • Mark workouts complete.

Plan fields identified in the sheet

Workout grouping/day, selected exercises, target muscle, prescribed rep range, sets, RIR (repetitions in reserve), instructions, and video links. Confirm these as the minimum plan fields.

Boundary: externally hosted video links only. No video uploading or hosting.

Decision required

The sheet contains weekly exercise-weight entries. Completion-only remains the baseline; confirm whether weight recording is essential to leaving the spreadsheet. Also confirm reusable plan templates versus individually assembled plans. Cardio logging is not included in this draft.

03

Food

Working proposal—subject to coach confirmation. The workbook connects individual foods, coach recipes, and personal recipes to daily logging.

Coach dashboard

  • Access and maintain the existing food catalog.
  • View client meal records and nutrition totals.
  • Provide food guidance; targets, prescribed plans, or both remain open.

Client mobile app

  • Select catalog foods and quantities.
  • Build and log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.
  • See calculated calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.
  • Review daily nutrition totals.
Decisions required

Confirm food guidance, the coach recipe library, and saving/reusing personal meals. Confirm catalog-maintenance permissions, quantity units, and how freely clients can adjust meals.

Boundary: no external food database, photo recognition, or client-created food records in the initial proposal. Creating a meal from catalog foods is distinct from adding a new food to the catalog.

04

Progress

Client mobile app

  • Submit dated weekly check-ins.
  • Record weight and agreed body measurements.
  • Add a short note, proposed.

Coach dashboard

  • Review check-in history and changes over time.
  • Review workout completion and food records alongside check-ins.
  • Use the records to adjust client plans.

Measurement fields found: chest, abdomen 5 cm above and below the navel, waist at the navel, hips, right and left thigh, and arm/biceps.

Decision required

Confirm which measurements are essential. Manual steps, workout-performance ratings, and lifestyle self-assessments exist in the sheet but are not automatically included.

05

Active-client migration

Included in Phase 1. Migrate only clients on a coach-approved active-client list. One workbook does not establish the complete client inventory or coach assignments.

  1. Review source sheets and approve field mappings.
  2. Import approved shared libraries without duplicating them per client.
  3. Import agreed active-client data and assign it to the correct coach.
  4. Validate counts, relationships, nutrition calculations, and video links.
  5. Resolve missing or invalid records, or obtain explicit acceptance of exceptions.
  6. Complete the final transfer before inviting migrated clients.

Preparation identified in the first-pass review

  • Confirm per-food nutrition units; some stored values appear per gram despite a per-100-gram header.
  • Extract embedded video URLs rather than just visible exercise names.
  • Identify test rows, invalid dates, duplicate entries, and incomplete records.
  • Map name-based relationships to stable identifiers.
  • Check whether other client workbooks follow this template.
Decision required

Current plans and latest measurements only, or additional historical records? Agree the source of truth, who prepares the data, and the final cutover process.

Original sheets remain unchanged as a reference. Inactive-client migration and ongoing Google Sheets synchronization are excluded.

06

Protect the first release

Explicitly outside Phase 1

  • Online payments.
  • FatSecret integration.
  • Photo-based calorie extraction.
  • AI suggestions.
  • In-app chat.
  • Individual set-by-set workout logging.
  • Progress-photo workflows and detailed cardio logging.
  • Wearable integrations and automatic step collection.
  • Inactive-client migration and ongoing sheet synchronization.

Not exclusions; pending decisions

Exercise-weight recording, coach recipes, saved personal meals, manual steps, additional check-in fields, reusable plan templates, and the amount of migration history.

One coaching business. Multiple coaches share the platform with assigned-client access. A multi-business SaaS platform is not part of this draft.

Later, based on actual use

Payments, FatSecret, photo calorie extraction, and AI suggestions are future candidates—not a fixed sequence or commitments for the next release.

08

Release acceptance criteria

These are release checks—not claims that the application has been built or verified.

  • Clients can register through email invitations.
  • Clients cannot access other clients’ data; coaches cannot access unassigned clients.
  • Assigned plans and updates appear for the correct client.
  • Client records persist and are visible to the assigned coach.
  • Nutrition calculations match coach-approved examples, including quantity units.
  • Agreed active-client data is imported accurately, with exceptions resolved or accepted.
  • The pilot completes the agreed weekly routine without maintaining a parallel spreadsheet.
09

Decisions for the meeting

  1. Validate the source: is this the current working template or a demonstration copy with older/test sections?
  2. Training: is exercise-weight recording essential, and are reusable plans needed?
  3. Food: targets, prescribed plans, recipe library, and saved personal meals—which are required?
  4. Progress: which measurements and optional daily/check-in fields are actively reviewed?
  5. Platforms: iOS, Android, or both; Arabic-first or bilingual?
  6. Migration: approve the active-client list, coach mapping, authoritative sources, history depth, and preparation owner.

Meeting outcome: approve the essential scope and resolve the open decisions before committing to development.
